# External Opportunity Search prompt construction

Use this reference when delegating bounded problem discovery to Pro, Deep
Research, another LLM, or a human reviewer. The external response is advisory
search evidence, never authorization to create a repository, run a Scout, or
claim novelty.

When multiple distinct decisions are ready, submit up to three asynchronous
Pro reviews through the shared dispatcher and continue local primary-source,
code, algebra or implementation work while they generate. Give every job a
unique decision-bound identity and one polling owner. Do not create duplicate
prompts merely to fill capacity.

For idea-stage cross-checking, keep the Pro pass genuinely independent:

- give Pro the frozen user/problem boundary and public-source date, but not the
  local candidate ranking or conclusions;
- run the local primary-source/code/algebra search in parallel;
- require each side to freeze its own causal fingerprints, direct/partial/
  generic-reduction map and unresolved uncertainties;
- merge only after both passes finish; disagreements trigger primary-source or
  algebra verification rather than majority voting.

For a theory or mathematical bottleneck, do not send a broad research prompt.
State one target proposition or derivation, its assumptions, the permitted
tools/evidence, the desired proof or counterexample, and the exact downstream
decision that the answer can change.

Every async submit must include the canonical `completion_thread_id` and
absolute `completion_cwd`, and its returned record must confirm that callback
binding. Store an absolute next-check due time; never reset it when a monitor
is retargeted.

## Compile from three layers

Construct each prompt as:

```text
stable research policy
+ current domain profile and closure ledger
+ request-specific objective, scope, date, venue, and budget
```

Keep stable policy in this skill. Keep changing paper neighborhoods, internal
outcomes, exact metrics, crowded fingerprints, and reopening conditions in the
workspace knowledge base or Program ledger. Keep the assembled dated prompt in
the workspace export or evidence directory. Do not hard-code a changing domain
state into this skill.

Before assembly, resolve:

- the authoritative domain profile or explicitly state that none exists;
- the evidence-freeze date and primary-source boundary;
- the user scope and hard exclusions;
- the previous Program state, consumed budget, and whether this request opens a
  substantively new Program or remains inside an active Epoch;
- the exact prior fingerprints that must not be revived;
- the intended artifact family and venue target, if any.

## Freeze the external-review contract

An external Opportunity Search prompt must state:

```text
search objective:
user boundary and hard exclusions:
evidence-freeze date:
primary-source contract:
raw-lead and counted-brief cap:
maximum active briefs:
maximum selected Scouts:
known scoped closures and reopening rules:
stage boundary:
required output fields:
legal terminal labels:
actions not authorized by the review:
```

For a venue-targeted innovation review, ask the reviewer to challenge both
stages separately: the bounded obvious-neighbor obligation before GPU queue
placement and the publication-grade novelty/irreducibility gate after a
material signal. A review may expose missing neighbors or reductions, but it
cannot certify novelty or acceptance probability.

Count the review against the active Program's external-review and attention
budget. A reviewer may recommend `probe`, `hold`, or `drop`; it cannot replenish
budgets or authorize code, data access, compute, or public-test use.

## Calibrate admission before search

For a broad-domain gate, require the reviewer to reconstruct at least three
retrospective positives at their pre-signal information states and three
obvious duplicates, vacuous cells, no-decision leads, or exact reductions. The
gate must admit every positive to a cheap problem witness and keep every
negative out of a probe.

Do not let publication-stage facts reject a plausible problem measurement.
Novelty, specificity, irreducibility, scalability, reviewer resistance, and
venue path become method-selection gates only after a material Problem Scout
signal. At Opportunity Search they may be named `challenge` fields that shape
the witness.

## Inject closures without poisoning recall

For every prior result, provide four fields:

```text
closed fingerprint:
decisive evidence or preserving reduction:
exact claim that is no longer admissible:
substantive change required for reopening:
```

Use closure records to stop renaming, carrier hopping, and selector/rank/seed
revival. Explicitly state that a scoped closure does not blacklist a topic or
keyword. A candidate may survive only by changing the target estimand, causal
bottleneck, information/deployment constraint, or actor-level decision with a
distinct prospective prediction.

Separate unrelated closures. Do not ask the reviewer to synthesize them into a
field-level negative, benchmark, or certificate unless the negative-synthesis
gate in `portfolio-search.md` has already passed.

## Separate divergent and convergent passes

The divergent pass generates raw actor/decision/loss transitions without
novelty, venue, reviewer, `probe`, `hold`, or `drop` labels. Do not require a
natural carrier, complete preserving composition, method kernel, specificity
proof, or paper path while generating.

The convergent pass grounds sources, merges causal fingerprints, and produces
bounded briefs. Each brief must report two independent states:

```text
problem_admission:
contribution_forecast:
```

The forecast cannot determine admission. A direct neighbor may make a method
contribution unlikely while the actor-level problem still deserves a cheap
measurement.

Also require `retention_state`: `PROBE_READY`, `EVIDENCE_GAP_LEAD`,
`BROADER_ARTIFACT_LEAD`, or `CLOSED`. Only exact/no-decision drops are closed.
Every hold or broader route must remain visible with one bounded next evidence
action or reopening fact, even when the current search budget exhausts.

## Request problem theses, not method names

Ask for raw leads across several problem spaces, then collapse them by causal
fingerprint into a bounded number of counted briefs. Each counted brief must
name:

1. affected actor and decision;
2. material actor-level loss and evidence status;
3. target estimand and minimum practical effect;
4. causal bottleneck and necessary support statistic;
5. information, cost, temporal, and deployment constraints;
6. nearest primary-source neighbor;
7. strongest generic or classical preserving reduction;
8. repair and contribution contrasts;
9. mechanism-deletion control;
10. adequate carrier and cheapest decision-complete witness;
11. positive, negative, and ambiguous actions;
12. provisional method residual only if the witness is positive;
13. cost, deadline, and strongest reviewer objection;
14. `problem_admission` and `contribution_forecast`.

Require labels such as `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, and `HYPOTHESIS`. A varying score,
taxonomy gap, missing keyword, formal ambiguity, larger oracle, or efficiency
gain is not an actor-level problem unless it reaches the target decision unit.

## Require joint feasibility before reduction closure

Do not accept “paper A + paper B + paper C + canary” as a preserving reduction
from component coverage alone. Before closing a problem, require one
joint-feasibility certificate for observables, operation order, extra
rendezvous, state/storage, objective/architecture compatibility, bytes,
latency, compute, deployment recipient, and an implementation/formal witness.
If a decision-critical field is unverified, label the composition a challenge
and include it in the Scout; do not use it as a pre-signal closure.

A source-grounded controlled carrier is admissible for scoped problem
existence. Require a natural carrier before the Scout only when natural
occurrence constitutes the actor, event timing, resource constraint, or
information asymmetry.

## Specify output and terminal semantics

Keep at most three active briefs and select at most one Problem Scout. Require
the selected route to include a frozen 1--3 day witness when feasible, not a
full method or publication matrix.

Use terminal labels that preserve scope. Recommended defaults:

```text
PROBE_WORTHY_METHOD_OPPORTUNITY_FOUND
ADMIT_TO_PROBLEM_SCOUT
HOLD_ONE_PRECISE_BLOCKER
DROP_PROBLEM_EXACT_REDUCTION
DROP_NO_DECISION
ROUTE_BROADER_ARTIFACT
SEARCH_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED_WITHOUT_SELECTION
```

`SEARCH_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED_WITHOUT_SELECTION` is an operational search terminal,
not `NO_STRONG_IDEA`, `NO_VIABLE_IDEA`, or a field-level `NO-GO`.

When the objective is a method paper, ask for a conditional paper path only
after the selected Scout design: one contribution sentence, provisional
mechanism or theorem target, decisive figure/table, strongest baseline,
confirmatory settings, and reviewer objection. Label all of it conditional on
a positive and attributed Scout.

## Validate and preserve the artifact

Start from `assets/opportunity-search-prompt-template.md`. Replace every
`{{PLACEHOLDER}}`, remove irrelevant optional sections, and run:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_opportunity_prompt.py /path/to/prompt.md
```

The validator checks structure only. It cannot verify scientific novelty,
source accuracy, closure correctness, candidate quality, or venue viability.

For a materially changed broad admission gate, copy
`assets/opportunity-gate-calibration-template.json`, fill at least three
retrospective positives and three negatives, and run:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_opportunity_gate_calibration.py /path/to/calibration.json
```

The calibration validator checks the decision table and false-admit/
false-reject counts. It does not prove that the historical reconstruction is
correct; preserve the source paths and pre-signal boundary for audit.

Preserve the assembled prompt with:

- date and version;
- domain profile and Program ledger paths;
- evidence-freeze date;
- hashes or commits when the workspace requires them;
- the external response and later adjudication as separate artifacts.

If the workspace knowledge base is dirty, add only the new prompt/profile and
non-overlapping navigation entries. Do not overwrite unrelated work merely to
make the prompt artifact clean.
